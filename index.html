What this model simplifies

  • Flat real investment returns every year — no market volatility or sequence-of-returns risk.
  • California tax brackets are an approximation, not the officially indexed 2026 table.
  • Modeled under federal filing status Single — confirm this matches your actual filing status.
  • Excludes real estate and any Social Security COLA beyond the embedded real-return assumption.
  • Social Security taxability uses a flat 85%-of-benefit inclusion rate rather than the tiered IRS formula.
